Product Design

Floating Shelf

  • Laser Cutting
  • CAD
  • Joinery

01 Problem

I had a bunch of figurines that I wanted to clear off my desk, but still display in my room. I wanted the shelf to be low-profile so that the focus was on my figurines.

02 How I made it

I designed the shelf in SolidWorks using organic splines and T-slot joints. Then I laser cut 0.25" clear acrylic in the Rapid Prototyping Lab at UPenn.
