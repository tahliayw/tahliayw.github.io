Light Box Figurine Shelf

  • Laser Cutting
  • CAD
  • Joinery

01 Description

For my Introduction to Mechanical Design Course (Spring 2025), I designed a lightbox in CAD that doubles as a shelf for my figurines. The goal was to combine aesthetics with functionality.

02 How I made it

I designed the lightbox in SolidWorks with T-slot joints. Then I laser cut parts out of 0.25" MDF in the Rapid Prototyping Lab at UPenn.
