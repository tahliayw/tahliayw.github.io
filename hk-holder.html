Hello Kitty Makeup Organizer

  • CAD
  • 3D Printing

01 Problem

  • My bathroom shelf is only 4 inches wide so my makeup products would frequently fall off into the sink or onto the floor.
  • I was only able to put 3-4 products on the shelf before it became too cluttered to be useful.
  • My products would sit in wet puddles on my shelf since it was so close to the sink.

03 How I made it

  • I designed the holder in SolidWorks, 3D printed it using ABS + dissolvable support material, and then I spray painted it for a more personalized color.
  • I designed it to utilize vertical space to maximize the number of products that could fit.
  • I measured all my products to customize the perfect for my makeup collection.
